# Stage153: isolate relative modality disagreement from common valence

2026-11-11. Follow-up DEVELOPMENT experiment informed by Stage151 (continuous supervision improves point MAE in six settings vs ordinary MTL, but incremental intervals mostly cross zero). All cohorts remain historical; retain every result.

The Stage151 cell-centered loss also penalizes errors shared by all three auxiliary modalities. That can couple the disagreement objective to common valence. Test a two-way residual projection, E=Uhat-U, D=P_cell E P_modality: first subtract each sample's mean error across its three modalities, then subtract the source/exact-overall-score cell mean vector. Penalize mean absolute D on multi-example FIT cells. This loss is invariant to any sample-specific common modality offset and any cell-specific modality bias. It is not a gradient orthogonality theorem and offers no no-harm/generalization guarantee. Two-way centering is a known mathematical operation, not itself claimed as newly invented.
